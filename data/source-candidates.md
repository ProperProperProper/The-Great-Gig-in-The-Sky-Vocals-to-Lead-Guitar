# Online source candidates

Checked on 18 October 2026. These links are research references, not permission to copy or redistribute their complete tabs. Confirm reuse rights before importing any source into `data/raw/` or publishing transformed material.

## Selection requirement

The project must produce an electric lead-guitar solo of Clare Torry's studio vocals. Chord accompaniments, piano reductions, acoustic fingerstyle arrangements, and guitar covers that replace the vocal melody are not acceptable as the primary transcription; they may only be used as secondary comparisons.

## Licensed purchase options

- URL: https://apps.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/pink-floyd/the-great-gig-in-the-sky/MN0102493_D2

## Harmonic and structural reference

### On the Path: Tracing Tonal Coherence in The Dark Side of the Moon

- URL: https://www.docdroid.net/file/download/yHM2YkV/odonnell-on-the-path-tracing-tonal-coherence-in-the-dark-side-of-the-moon-pdf.pdf
- Content: an academic formal and tonal analysis.
- Relevant findings: vocals enter with the band at measure 17 (about 1:07); the central section uses an `i7–IV9` G Dorian vamp; the vocal range is roughly a thirteenth; prominent climax tones are G5 and neighboring A.
- Project use: validates separate G Dorian highlighting and provides structural checkpoints for transcription review.

## Recommended working method

1. Use the Songsterr Clare Torry track as the main visual reference.
2. Compare disputed pitches and playable positions with the original recording by ear.
3. Use GuitarTwitt and Guitar Tab Creator only as independent cross-checks.
4. Encode notes from an independently verified project transcription rather than copying a site's complete presentation.
5. Keep source attribution and permission status with every imported artifact.
